# **1\. About this policy**

## **1.1 Purpose**

This Account Deletion Policy (the "Policy") explains how Techforge Developers Limited ("Lintel," "we," "us," or "our") handles requests to close accounts and to delete personal data from the Lintel platform. The Policy sets out:

* Who can request deletion and what they can request;

* What we delete, what we keep, and why;

* How long deletion takes and how we confirm it;

* How we balance individual deletion rights against legal, regulatory, and operational obligations to retain certain data.

## **1.2 Status**

This Policy forms part of the Lintel Terms of Service and is incorporated by reference into the Privacy Policy, the Data Retention Policy, and the Data Processing Addendum. Capitalised terms used but not defined in this Policy have the meanings given in the Terms or Privacy Policy.

## **1.3 Why this matters**

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA"), the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR, and other applicable data protection laws, individuals have certain rights to have their personal data erased. As a data controller in respect of our direct customers and a data processor in respect of end-client data, we treat these rights seriously and operate clear, time-bound processes to honour them.

## **1.4 Our roles**

This Policy distinguishes between three different roles:

* Customer: A real estate agency, broker, or other organization that has a Lintel account.

* Authorized User: An individual the Customer has invited to use its Lintel account (employees, agents, accountants, external marketers).

* End Client: An individual (such as a tenant, buyer, guest, or vendor) whose personal data is processed through the Service by a Customer.

Different deletion rights and processes apply to each role, as set out below.

**Plain English.** If you're a Lintel customer and you want to close your account, you can — and we'll delete your data within 90 days, except for what we're legally required to keep. If you're an end client of an agency and want your data deleted, contact the agency first; we'll support them in honouring your request as their data processor.

# **2\. Who can request deletion**

## **2.1 Customer account holders**

The primary account holder (the named owner of a Customer account) may request:

* Closure of the Customer account in its entirety;

* Deletion of all Customer Data associated with the account;

* Removal of specific Authorized Users from the account;

* Deletion of specific records (clients, properties, contracts, bookings) within the account.

## **2.2 Authorized Users**

Individual Authorized Users may request:

* Removal of their own Authorized User access from a Customer account (they remain bound to ask the Customer to remove them from the Customer's own records);

* Deletion of personal data about themselves that Lintel holds as a data controller (such as login records, support communications, and engagement analytics);

* Withdrawal of consent for any optional processing to which they had previously consented.

Authorized Users cannot, by themselves, delete records belonging to the Customer account they were invited to. Such deletion is the Customer's responsibility.

## **2.3 End Clients**

End Clients have rights under the NDPA, GDPR, and similar laws to request deletion of their personal data. Since the Customer (the agency) is the data controller of End Client data and Lintel is the data processor, the primary place for End Clients to direct deletion requests is the relevant Customer.

If an End Client cannot reach the Customer, or has reason to believe the Customer is not honouring its obligations, they may contact us at privacy@getlintel.org. In that case, we will:

* Acknowledge the request and confirm receipt;

* Where lawfully possible, identify the relevant Customer and forward the request;

* Where the Customer fails to respond, exercise our processor obligations to assist;

* Take direct action only where Applicable Law or the DPA requires it of us as processor.

## **2.4 Authorities and others**

We may also act on:

* Lawful orders from a court, regulator, or other competent authority;

* Requests from a legal representative, guardian, or attorney with appropriate authority documents;

* Requests from estate administrators where the data subject has died (where lawful);

* Cooperation requests from other data controllers in line with the GDPR's notification provisions where applicable.

# **3\. What gets deleted**

## **3.1 On Customer account closure**

When a Customer closes its account (and after the 30-day Retrieval Period set out in the Terms), we delete or anonymize:

| Category | Examples |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Account details** | Account name, agency information, billing address, settings, branding, custom domains. |
| **Authorized User records** | Names, email addresses, roles, login history of users invited by the Customer (subject to their own rights — see Section 6). |
| **Client records** | Tenants, buyers, guests, vendors, and prospects uploaded by the Customer. |
| **Property records** | Listings, photographs, descriptions, status records, title documents. |
| **Contracts** | Drafts, sent and signed contracts, attachments — subject to e-signing audit trail retention (Section 15). |
| **Bookings** | Shortlet bookings, calendar entries, house rules acceptance records. |
| **Payments** | Payment records, installment schedules, receipts — subject to financial-records retention (Section 16). |
| **Communications** | Email content, engagement records, internal notes, support tickets. |
| **Files and attachments** | Uploaded documents, images, PDFs, signature images. |
| **Custom configuration** | Contract templates, email templates, custom fields, integrations. |

## **3.2 On End Client deletion request**

When an End Client requests deletion (typically through the relevant Customer), the following categories of personal data are deleted or anonymized:

* Identity data: name, contact details, identification document data;

* Communications: emails and messages exchanged with the End Client;

* Activity data: engagement records, login records, booking activity;

* Free-text references: removal or redaction of mentions of the End Client in notes, messages, or other unstructured fields where reasonably identifiable.

Personal data about the End Client may be retained where retention is necessary for legitimate purposes that override the deletion right, as set out in Section 4\.

## **3.3 On Authorized User self-removal**

When an Authorized User asks Lintel to delete the personal data we hold about them as data controller, we delete:

* Their Lintel login credentials and authentication tokens;

* Their account profile (display name, avatar, preferences);

* Their personal usage analytics and product engagement records;

* Their support correspondence with Lintel;

* Their consent records for optional features.

Records of the Authorized User's actions within the Customer's workspace (such as which contracts they sent, which payments they recorded) are part of the Customer's audit log and remain available to the Customer for as long as the Customer's account is active. The Customer is the data controller of those records, and we cannot unilaterally delete them on the Authorized User's request — the Authorized User should direct that request to the Customer.

# **4\. What we cannot delete**

In a small number of cases, we are unable or unwilling to delete certain data even where deletion is requested. This Section lists those cases and the reasons.

## **4.1 Legally required retention**

Some data we are required by Applicable Law to retain for specific periods. We will retain this data for the required period and delete it thereafter. Examples include:

| Category | Reason for retention | Typical period |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Financial transaction records** | FIRS / Companies Income Tax record-keeping requirements | 6 years from transaction |
| **Contract audit trails** | Evidence Act and limitation period for written contracts | 10 years from signing |
| **KYC and AML records** | Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 | 5 years after relationship ends |
| **Suspicious activity records** | NFIU / regulatory reporting obligations | As required by law |
| **Subscription / billing records** | Tax law, dispute resolution | 6 years from charge |
| **Data subject rights request records** | NDPA accountability requirements | 3-5 years from request |
| **Court-ordered preservation** | Compliance with court order | As ordered |

## **4.2 Legitimate interests overriding deletion**

In limited cases, our or a third party's legitimate interests in retaining data override an individual's deletion right. Examples include:

* Defending against legal claims threatened or in progress: we may retain relevant data until claims are resolved or time-barred;

* Establishing or exercising legal rights: we may retain data needed to demonstrate that an obligation was performed or that a representation was made;

* Detecting and preventing fraud or abuse: we may retain identifying information about persons we have reasonable grounds to suspect of fraud, abuse, or other unlawful activity, to protect ourselves and other users from repeat conduct;

* Protecting other data subjects' rights: where deletion of one person's data would inappropriately compromise another's rights or interests.

## **4.3 Aggregated and anonymized data**

Data that has been aggregated or de-identified to the point where it no longer relates to an identified or identifiable natural person is no longer personal data under the NDPA or GDPR. Such data may be retained indefinitely and continues to be used for analytics, benchmarking, and Service improvement. Deletion requests do not require us to delete properly anonymized data.

## **4.4 Data with multiple data subjects**

Some records contain personal data about multiple people. For example, a signed tenancy contract typically contains data about:

* The tenant (the End Client typically requesting deletion);

* The landlord or vendor;

* Any guarantors or witnesses;

* The Customer's staff who sent or processed the contract.

Where deletion would compromise the rights of other parties to such a record (such as the landlord's right to evidence of a tenancy), we may:

* Decline to delete the record, but

* Redact or pseudonymize the requesting party's data within the record where technically feasible; or

* Restrict access to the record to specific lawful purposes.

## **4.5 Backups and disaster recovery copies**

Customer Data persists in encrypted backups for a rolling period (typically 90 days). On request for deletion:

* We delete the data from production systems within the timelines in Section 10;

* The data continues to exist in backups until they are overwritten in the normal course (no later than 90 days);

* Restoration of backups would not re-introduce deleted data into production — we have controls to filter restorations against the deletion log.

## **4.6 Free-text and unstructured references**

Where personal data appears as free-text in unstructured fields (notes, comments, contract clauses, message bodies) referencing a person whose data is to be deleted, we make reasonable efforts to remove or redact identifying references but cannot guarantee complete removal in every instance. We will explain this limitation to requesters and document the efforts taken.

# **5\. Customer-initiated account closure**

## **5.1 How to close your account**

Customers can close their Lintel account through any of the following channels:

* In-product: "Settings" → "Account" → "Close account" (for the primary account holder);

* By email: send a closure request to billing@getlintel.org from the email address associated with the account, identifying the account and confirming the closure;

* By post: written request to Techforge Developers Limited, 27 Carter Street, Ebute Metta, Lagos.

We may require additional verification (Section 14\) to confirm your authority to close the account, especially where it is a multi-user account or where the request comes from an email address other than the registered primary email.

## **5.2 What happens immediately**

On confirming your closure request:

* We disable auto-renewal of your subscription;

* We send a confirmation email outlining the steps and timeline that follow;

* We grant you the 30-day Retrieval Period under the Terms during which your account remains in read-only mode for data export;

* We notify your Authorized Users of the impending closure (we do not give them rights to override the closure, but they should be aware so they can take their own action).

## **5.3 What happens during the Retrieval Period**

During the 30 days following confirmation:

* Your data remains intact and accessible to you for export;

* You can download Customer Data using the export tools in the app;

* You can also request a full data export by email (see Section 11);

* New activities (creating contracts, sending emails, taking bookings) are typically disabled to preserve a clean snapshot;

* You can change your mind and reactivate the account during this period (see Section 17).

## **5.4 What happens at the end of the Retrieval Period**

At the end of the 30 days:

* Customer Data is deleted from production systems within a further 60 days, in line with our standard deletion timelines (Section 10);

* The account is marked as closed in our records;

* We retain records subject to legal retention obligations (Section 4.1);

* We send a final confirmation email summarizing what was deleted and what was retained, with reasons.

## **5.5 Outstanding obligations**

Closure does not relieve you of outstanding obligations under the Terms, including:

* Unpaid Fees (which become immediately payable on closure);

* Indemnification obligations for events arising before closure;

* Confidentiality obligations;

* Provisions of the Terms that by their nature survive termination.

# **6\. Authorized user removal**

## **6.1 Removal by the Customer**

Customers can remove Authorized Users at any time through the in-product user management screens. When a Customer removes an Authorized User:

* The user's access to the account is immediately revoked;

* The user's profile within the Customer's account is retained as part of the Customer's audit trail (so that historical records of who created or modified records remain accurate);

* The user's authentication credentials are deactivated;

* Active sessions are terminated.

## **6.2 Self-removal by an Authorized User**

An Authorized User can ask Lintel to:

* Remove their account access entirely from a specific Customer's workspace;

* Delete personal data Lintel holds about them as data controller.

Self-removal does not, however, remove records of the user's actions within Customer workspaces, which remain part of the Customer's audit trail.

## **6.3 Departing staff**

When a member of your staff leaves your agency, we strongly recommend that you:

* Promptly remove their Authorized User access;

* Reassign any client, contract, or property ownership records to current staff;

* Review any external communications they had sent to ensure continuity;

* Update any pre-uploaded signature images if the departed person had authorized signing for your agency.

## **6.4 Effect on email engagement**

Where an Authorized User has been sending emails through Lintel under their own name, removal of the user does not retract emails already sent. Subsequent engagement (opens, clicks) by recipients will continue to be tracked against the original send, even after the user has been removed.

# **7\. End client erasure requests**

## **7.1 First port of call: the Customer**

If you are an End Client of an agency that uses Lintel (for example, a tenant, buyer, or shortlet guest) and you wish to have your personal data deleted, the first step is to contact the agency directly. The agency is the data controller of your personal data; Lintel is the data processor. The agency is responsible for:

* Verifying your identity;

* Assessing whether deletion is required under Applicable Law;

* Considering any legitimate interests that override the deletion right;

* Instructing Lintel as data processor.

## **7.2 When to contact Lintel directly**

You may contact Lintel directly at privacy@getlintel.org if:

* The agency has stopped operating or is unreachable;

* The agency has refused or failed to respond to your request within a reasonable time (typically 30 days);

* You cannot identify which agency holds your data;

* You believe the agency is processing your data unlawfully and you want to escalate.

## **7.3 What we do on receiving a direct request**

On receiving an End Client request directly:

* We acknowledge receipt within 5 working days;

* We try to identify the relevant Customer and forward the request to them, asking them to respond;

* We give the Customer a reasonable time (typically 14 working days) to respond;

* Where the Customer does not respond, we exercise our processor obligations and take such action as the DPA and Applicable Law allow us to take, including, where appropriate, deleting or restricting access to the personal data.

## **7.4 Customer instructions**

Where the Customer instructs us as processor to delete End Client data:

* We will delete or anonymize the personal data within 30 days of the instruction (or such shorter period as Applicable Law requires);

* We will confirm completion to the Customer;

* We will preserve any data subject to legal retention obligations (Section 4\) and inform the Customer accordingly.

## **7.5 Disputing a Customer's refusal**

If you believe the Customer's refusal to delete your data is unlawful:

* You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at www.ndpc.gov.ng;

* If you are in the EU or UK, you may complain to your national data protection authority (a list is available at edpb.europa.eu);

* You may seek civil remedies through the courts.

Lintel cannot adjudicate disputes between Customers and End Clients; we take instructions as processor only from the Customer (the controller), except where Applicable Law requires us to take direct action.

# **8\. Inactive account closure**

## **8.1 Inactivity threshold**

Accounts that have been inactive for an extended period are subject to closure by us. Inactivity means:

* No user login;

* No API activity;

* No new records created;

* No incoming or outgoing emails sent through the Service.

The default inactivity threshold is 12 months for paid Plans (where the subscription has lapsed) and 12 months for free-tier accounts.

## **8.2 Notification before closure**

Before closing an inactive account, we send notification:

* 90 days before closure: first reminder by email, with instructions on how to re-activate or export data;

* 60 days before closure: second reminder;

* 30 days before closure: final notice;

* Where we have multiple email addresses on file, we send to all of them.

## **8.3 Re-activation**

If you re-activate before the closure date by logging in, paying any outstanding Fees, or contacting us, the closure is cancelled. Your data is preserved in full.

## **8.4 Closure of inactive accounts**

If the closure date passes without re-activation:

* The account is closed;

* The same deletion process as a Customer-initiated closure applies (Section 5);

* Data is deleted on the timeline set out in Section 10\.

# **9\. Account closure for breach**

## **9.1 Closure as enforcement**

We may close an account as an enforcement action where the Customer has materially breached the Terms, the Acceptable Use Policy, or any other Lintel policy, in accordance with Section 15 of the Terms. Closure for breach:

* May be without the 30-day Retrieval Period (for serious breaches);

* May include an immediate freeze of the account pending investigation;

* May involve preserving data for evidence rather than deletion (Section 4.2).

## **9.2 Customer Data treatment for breach closure**

Where we close an account for breach:

* We will, where lawful, give you a brief retrieval period (typically 14 days) to export Customer Data;

* We may decline a retrieval period entirely where Applicable Law requires preservation or where the Customer's continued possession of the data would create further risk;

* After the retrieval period (if any), data is deleted in accordance with Section 10, subject to legal retention requirements.

## **9.3 Right to appeal**

You may appeal a closure for breach under Section 17 of the Acceptable Use Policy. Pending the appeal, the closure may be paused or the data preserved, at our discretion.

# **10\. Deletion timeline**

## **10.1 Standard timeline**

For a Customer-initiated account closure, the standard deletion timeline is:

| Stage | Period | What happens |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Confirmation** | Day 0 | Closure request received; confirmation email sent; auto-renewal disabled. |
| **Retrieval Period** | Days 1-30 | Account in read-only mode; Customer can export data; closure can be cancelled. |
| **Production deletion** | Days 31-90 | Customer Data deleted from production databases, file storage, and search indexes; deletion log created. |
| **Backup purge** | Days 1-90 | Backups containing deleted data are overwritten in the normal rolling backup cycle (typically within 90 days). |
| **Sub-processor propagation** | Days 1-30 (after Day 30\) | Deletion instructions transmitted to relevant Sub-processors (Section 13). |
| **Confirmation of deletion** | By Day 120 | Final email confirming deletion completion and any retained categories with reasons. |

## **10.2 Faster timelines**

In some circumstances, deletion may be expedited:

* End Client erasure requests that the Customer or Lintel determines are valid: completed within 30 days from the date of instruction;

* Court or regulator orders requiring expedited deletion: completed within the period required by the order;

* Customer-initiated closure of accounts with little or no Customer Data (such as accounts that were never meaningfully used): may be completed earlier.

## **10.3 Slower timelines**

In some circumstances, deletion may take longer than the standard timeline:

* Where verification of the requester's identity or authority takes time;

* Where deletion would compromise other parties' rights (Section 4.4) and we need to consult before proceeding;

* Where the account is unusually large or complex and bulk deletion needs to be coordinated;

* Where legal retention obligations require categorization of data before deletion;

* Where the request is one of many in a backlog (we process in good faith and in chronological order).

Where we exceed the standard timeline by more than 30 days, we will notify you of the reason and expected completion date.

# **11\. Data export before deletion**

## **11.1 Self-service export**

During the Retrieval Period and at any time before then, Customers can export Customer Data using:

* CSV exports of clients, properties, contracts, bookings, payments (available from the relevant module);

* PDF downloads of signed contracts and audit trails;

* Bulk file downloads of uploaded documents and attachments;

* API exports (where the Plan includes API access);

* Email export of templates, scheduled communications, and engagement data.

## **11.2 Assisted export**

Where the self-service tools are insufficient (such as where access has already been terminated, or where you need data in a specific format), email us at privacy@getlintel.org with details of what you need. We will:

* Acknowledge the request within 5 working days;

* Provide an estimate of the work involved and any cost-based fee;

* Deliver the export within 30 days of agreement on scope;

* Provide the data in commonly used machine-readable formats.

## **11.3 Export costs**

Self-service exports are free. Assisted exports are typically free for routine requests but may incur a cost-based fee for:

* Exports outside the standard formats;

* Exports requiring substantial engineering work to assemble;

* Repeated exports of the same data set;

* Exports of data subject to legal retention exceptions where bespoke filtering is required.

## **11.4 What is included in an export**

A complete data export typically includes:

* All structured records (clients, properties, contracts, bookings, payments) in CSV;

* All uploaded documents and attachments in their original formats;

* Signed contracts and their audit trails as PDF;

* Account configuration, templates, and settings as JSON;

* Email send history and engagement records as CSV;

* A README describing the format and structure of the export.

## **11.5 What is not included**

Exports do not generally include:

* Lintel's internal system logs, security logs, and operational telemetry;

* Aggregated analytics that do not identify your account;

* Data of other Customers (which would be a serious breach);

* Internal Lintel personnel notes about your account (such as support troubleshooting notes).

# **12\. Backups and residual copies**

## **12.1 Backup retention**

We maintain backups of the production database and file storage for:

* Disaster recovery: protecting against catastrophic data loss;

* Operational recovery: restoring from accidental data corruption or deletion;

* Regulatory and contractual obligations: preserving evidence as required.

Backups are encrypted at rest and stored across multiple geographic regions. The rolling backup window is typically 90 days; older backups are overwritten or deleted.

## **12.2 Deletion from backups**

When we delete data from production:

* The data persists in backups for up to 90 days, until backups are overwritten in the normal course;

* We do not actively scrub backups for deleted data, because doing so would compromise backup integrity for the data that should remain;

* We maintain a deletion log so that, if a backup is restored, deleted data is not re-introduced into production;

* Backups containing data that should have been deleted are not retrieved or accessed except in extraordinary circumstances (and then only with strict controls).

## **12.3 Restoration policy**

If we ever need to restore from backup:

* The restoration is filtered against the deletion log to exclude data that has been deleted;

* Where filtering is not technically practicable, the affected data is re-deleted from production immediately after restoration;

* End Clients and Customers whose deleted data was temporarily re-instated are notified where required by Applicable Law (such as a personal data breach notification).

## **12.4 Residual copies**

Some residual copies may exist for limited periods in:

* Caches and content-delivery network edge nodes (typically purged within hours);

* Search indexes (re-indexed periodically);

* Email-delivery provider logs (subject to their own retention);

* System logs (typically retained for 12 months for security purposes).

We use reasonable efforts to ensure that residual copies are purged within the times described in this Section and our Data Retention Policy.

# **13\. Sub-processors and third parties**

## **13.1 Notifying Sub-processors**

When data is deleted from Lintel's systems, the deletion also needs to propagate to any Sub-processors that hold the data. The Sub-processors currently used by Lintel include (see Subprocessor Disclosure for the full list):

* Supabase (database, storage, authentication);

* Cloudflare (CDN, file delivery);

* Resend (email delivery);

* Flutterwave (payment processing);

* Google (analytics, authentication where used);

* Apple (App Store distribution where used);

* Sentry or similar (error reporting where used).

## **13.2 How propagation works**

When a deletion is processed in Lintel:

* Data in Supabase is deleted from the database and storage immediately, then propagated through Supabase's own backup retention;

* Data delivered via Cloudflare CDN is invalidated from caches;

* Email-sending data is retained by Resend for their own log-retention period (typically up to 30 days), after which it is deleted;

* Payment records are retained by Flutterwave for their own retention periods governed by Nigerian financial regulations;

* Analytics data is anonymized or removed from Google Analytics as instructed.

## **13.3 Limitations**

We do not control the internal systems of Sub-processors. Where a Sub-processor's own retention policies require longer retention than Lintel's, that retention applies and is outside our control. The Sub-processor Disclosure summarizes each Sub-processor's role and key retention practices.

# **14\. Verification and identity checks**

## **14.1 Why we verify**

To prevent unauthorized deletion (which could itself be a serious breach), we verify the identity and authority of anyone requesting account closure or data deletion.

## **14.2 Standard verification**

For most requests, we verify identity by:

* Confirming the request was sent from the email address associated with the account;

* Cross-checking against recent activity in the account;

* Where applicable, requiring the requester to confirm via a link sent to that email address.

## **14.3 Additional verification**

Where the request is unusual, high-impact, or from an email not associated with the account, we may require additional verification, such as:

* Copy of an identification document (national ID, passport, driver's licence);

* Confirmation of recent activity or specific account details;

* Verification call to a phone number associated with the account;

* Where the request is from a legal representative: power of attorney or other authority document.

## **14.4 Acting on unverified requests**

We will not act on a deletion or closure request that we cannot reasonably verify. If verification cannot be completed:

* We will notify the requester of the verification we need;

* We will pause processing for up to 30 days pending verification;

* If verification is not provided within that period, we will close the request without action.

## **14.5 Free of charge**

Verification is generally free of charge. We may charge a reasonable fee only where requests are manifestly unfounded, repetitive, or excessive, in line with the NDPA and GDPR.

# **15\. Effect on signed documents**

## **15.1 Why signed contracts are special**

Signed contracts (and their associated audit trails) are a special category for deletion purposes because:

* They evidence legal obligations between parties;

* They are subject to the limitation period for written contracts (10 years under most Nigerian state Limitation Laws);

* They may be needed as evidence in litigation, arbitration, or regulatory proceedings;

* Their deletion could prejudice the rights of other parties to the contract.

## **15.2 Default retention**

By default, signed contracts and their audit trails are retained for 10 years from the date of signing, regardless of account closure status, in line with the Lintel Electronic Signature Agreement and the Data Retention Policy.

## **15.3 Deletion on request**

Where you request deletion of signed contracts before the 10-year period expires:

* We will consider the request in light of the rights of other parties to the contract;

* We may consult the other parties (the End Clients who signed) before deleting;

* We may decline to delete where doing so would prejudice the rights of other parties or our own legal interests;

* Where deletion is permitted, we will delete the document and the audit trail, leaving only a residual record that the deletion occurred (date, requester, contract reference).

## **15.4 End Client-initiated deletion of signed documents**

Where an End Client requests deletion of a signed contract:

* Their request typically goes to the Customer (the controller) first;

* The Customer assesses whether deletion is required, and consults Lintel as processor;

* Lintel will generally retain the document and audit trail for the 10-year period, in line with our retention policy and the legitimate interests of the other parties;

* Where the End Client establishes that deletion is required notwithstanding our retention interest, we will discuss with the Customer and decide on a course of action.

# **16\. Effect on financial records**

## **16.1 Why financial records are special**

Financial transaction records — including payment receipts, invoices, installment schedules, commission records, and tax invoices — are subject to specific retention obligations under Nigerian tax law, in particular:

* Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) requirements for record-keeping;

* Companies Income Tax Act requirements;

* VAT Act record-keeping obligations;

* Equivalent state-level tax authority requirements.

These obligations generally require retention of records for at least 6 years from the relevant transaction.

## **16.2 Default retention**

By default, financial records associated with your account are retained for 6 years from the relevant transaction date, regardless of account closure status. This applies to:

* Subscription invoices and payment records between you and Lintel;

* Records of payments you have recorded on your behalf in respect of your End Clients (such as rent payments, installment payments, commission payments);

* Tax invoices issued through the Service;

* Commission tracking and pay-out records.

## **16.3 Deletion of financial records**

On account closure:

* The above records are retained for the 6-year period from each relevant transaction;

* During the retention period, the records may be inaccessible to you through the user interface (since the account is closed) but remain in our archived storage;

* On expiry of the retention period, the records are deleted in accordance with our standard deletion processes;

* On reasonable request during the retention period, we will provide an export of your financial records for your own tax or dispute purposes (subject to verification under Section 14).

## **16.4 Tax-related obligations**

# **17\. Re-opening a closed account**

## **17.1 Within the Retrieval Period**

During the 30-day Retrieval Period:

* You can cancel the closure at any time by contacting us at billing@getlintel.org or by signing in to your account and following the cancellation flow;

* Auto-renewal is re-enabled if you confirm payment;

## **17.2 After deletion has begun**

Once the Retrieval Period has ended and deletion has started:

* Most data is no longer recoverable;

* A new account with the same name or email address can be created, but the old data is gone;

* Limited recovery from backups may be possible in extraordinary circumstances within the backup window (typically 90 days), at our discretion and at your cost;

## **17.3 Reactivating a closed account**

If you want to use Lintel again after closure:

* You can create a fresh account at any time;

* You will need to re-enter your account details, invite Authorized Users, configure templates, and import data;

* We will not associate the new account with the closed account unless you specifically request it for billing-history or audit purposes;

* If your previous account was closed for breach (Section 9), we may decline to allow you to create a new account.

# **18\. Disputes about deletion**

## **18.1 Where we decline to delete**

Where we decline to delete data — typically because of a legal retention obligation, an override under Section 4, or the rights of other parties — we will:

* Explain in writing why we have declined;

* Identify the specific categories of data that have been retained;

* Identify the period for which we will retain them;

* Confirm when they will be deleted at the end of that period;

* Explain any rights you have to challenge our decision.

## **18.2 Where you disagree with our retention**

If you believe we should have deleted data we have retained, you may:

* Email privacy@getlintel.org with a full explanation;

* Request escalation to our Data Protection Officer (dpo@getlintel.org);

* Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or your local supervisory authority if you are in the EU or UK;

* Seek civil remedies through the courts.

## **18.3 Where we delete in error**

If we delete data in error:

* We will inform you as soon as practicable;

* We will attempt restoration from backup (if available and lawful);

* We will notify any third parties whose interests are affected, where appropriate;

* We will treat the incident as a data incident under our Incident Response Policy.

## **18.4 Where we delete despite your objection**

If we delete data despite your objection (because we are legally required to do so, or because retention would itself be unlawful):

* We will give you reasonable notice before deletion;

* We will allow you a chance to export any data lawfully exportable;

* We will document the legal basis for the deletion.
